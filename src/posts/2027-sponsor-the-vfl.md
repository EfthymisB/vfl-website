Three seasons in, the VFL has grown from a few studio sides having a kickabout into a proper
league: ten teams, a full fixture list, a cup and an awards night that ends
somewhere nobody can quite remember.

Season 2027 kicks off in February, and we're looking for sponsors to come along for the ride.

## Who you'd be reaching

The league is made up of teams from studios across London's visual effects and Animation industry. In 2026 that was:

And it's not just the artists. Squads are full of production staff, pipeline and R&D people,
IT, recruiters and the odd head of department who still thinks they've got the legs. That's well over
a hundred players, plus the colleagues who follow the table, turn up on the touchline and come to
the awards night.

They follow the league week in, week out: results on [Sofascore]({{ site.social.sofascore }}), news
on this site, and match photos and banter on [Instagram]({{ site.social.instagram }}) and
[LinkedIn]({{ site.social.linkedin }}).

## What we can do together

- **Put your name on the silverware.** Sponsor the league itself, the cup or
  one of the season awards. (The Golden Sieve is available. Nobody else wants it either.)
- **Host the big nights.** Back the season launch or the end-of-season awards night, and be the
  name on everyone's lips (and on the bar tab).
- **Get seen online.** Your logo and link on this website, from the home page to the footer, plus
  shout-outs on our Instagram and LinkedIn through the season.
- **Meet the people you want to hire.** If you're recruiting in the industry, there's no better
  place to find a compositor who can also track back.

We've got packages for different budgets, from a single award to the whole season, and we're
happy to shape something around what you want.

## Who it suits

Anyone who wants London's VFX and Animation community to know who they are: software and
hardware companies, render and cloud services, recruiters, training providers, studios that
fancy showing off, and the pubs and businesses around our pitches.

## Where the money goes

The VFL is run by volunteers and we keep it as cheap as we can for the teams. Sponsorship pays for
the extras that make it feel like a proper league: trophies, the awards night, socials during the
season, and keeping the entry fee down for everyone.
