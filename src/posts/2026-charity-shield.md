The season curtain-raiser finally happened on 26 February, seven weeks after it was originally
meant to — and **FC ILM** made short work of it, beating **FC ETC** 3–0 to take the first
VFX Football League Charity Shield.

The fixture pits the reigning league champions against the cup holders. ILM arrived as back-to-back
Division 1 winners; ETC as the side that lifted the inaugural VFL Cup with a 2–1 win over Escape
the previous August, having gone through a 6–0 semi-final on the way.

The Shield now becomes an annual thing: champions against cup winners, played before the league
programme starts, on whichever January evening the pitch and the weather both cooperate.
