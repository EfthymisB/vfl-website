Hello everyone! Last night we wrapped up the 2026 season with our end-of-season awards night. Thanks to everyone who came along. Here's who went home with what.

## Sinner 🏆

![Sinner](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/sinner.jpg)

The ‘Sinner’ award was accepted by **Craig** on behalf of **Kit**, who couldn't make it on account of too many bookings.

Surprisingly one of the nicest players in the league, and its social media manager, Kit loves a word with the referee. This season that landed him in the disciplinary book, and made him our 2026 Sinner!

## One Club Man 🏆

![One club man](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/one_club_man.jpg)

The ‘One Club Man’ award, or more likely the bad smell award.

He left the company four years ago, but he's managed to hang on to his place and is still repping {% team "oou" %} to this day: the one and only **Tom Cowlishaw**!

## Golden Sieve 🏆

![Golden sieve](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/golden_sieve.jpg)

The worst defence in the league belongs to the {% logo "cs" %} Sinners. But their attendance last night was as leaky as their defence, so runners-up {% team "ilm" %} accepted the award!

Congrats, {% logo "ilm" %}: joint second-worst defence!

## Journeyman 🏆

![Journeyman](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/journeyman.jpg)

The VFL has only been around for three seasons, but somehow these lads have played for a new team in every one of them!

**Hamish** and **Matty** both started their footballing careers at {% team "jellyfish" %}’s Stingers. Jellyfish is a company that went bust and rose from the ashes as JFX, all before Man City's 115 charges have been dealt with.

From there, **Hamish** moved to champions {% logo "ilm" %} and won the league in his second season, while **Matty** joined title contenders {% team "etc" %}.

For his third move in three years, Hamish joined title hopefuls {% team "dneg" %}, who believed they'd found the missing piece of the puzzle to take them over the line. Lightning didn't strike twice.

Instead, it was Matty who joined the champions this time, linking up with {% logo "oou" %}. The only question left: where will these two be playing their football in 2027?

## Fair Play 🏆

![Fair play](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/fair_play.jpg)

{% team "ilm" %} took this one home. Fair play to them for being so committed to letting someone else have a go at winning the league: they almost finished below a team that stopped playing with three weeks to go.

## Playmaker 🏆

![Playmaker](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/playmaker.jpg)

This season's Playmaker award goes to **Jacob Leslie**, whose five assists set him apart from the rest.

## Best Defence 🏆

![Best defence](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/best_defence.jpg)

The ‘bridesmaids’ of the season, who fought tooth and nail right down to the final day: {% team "bk" %} had the best defence in the league, conceding just 8 goals all season!

## Golden Boot 🏆

![Golden boot](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/golden_boot.jpg)

The Golden Boot, and it wasn't even close!

With a fantastic **13** goals, {% team "escape" %}'s **Leo Wing** soared to the top of the scoring charts.

## Player of the Season (POTS) 🏆

![POTS](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/pots.jpg)

The 2026 POTS had to go to **Leo Wing** of {% logo "escape" %}!

Not only was he the league's top scorer, he also finished runner-up in the assists table, behind his teammate **Jacob**.

The third-year student graduates from Escape this summer, and if his off-the-field skills are anywhere near his on-the-field ones, studios will be fighting over his signature.

## VFL 2026 Winners 🏆

![VFL 2026 Winners](/assets/img/awards_night_2026/vfl_awards_night_cover.jpg)

And last but certainly not least, our 2026 league winners: {% team "oou" %}!

After finishing at the bottom of the Division 1 league last season, The Owls reinvented themselves, with brand-new kits and the kind of understanding that only comes from playing together for three seasons.

The title was anything but simple. It came down to the final day, when {% logo "oou" %} pipped {% team "bk" %} on Goals For.

Congratulations, {% team "oou" %}, on a fantastic 2026. We can't wait to see The Owls defend their title in 2027!
