For two and a bit seasons the VFX Football League has run on Sofascore, Instagram and an
increasingly unmanageable set of WhatsApp threads. It worked. It also meant that anyone who
wasn't already in the loop had no way of finding out what this thing actually was.

So: a website.

## What's on it

**[Live standings](/table/)** and **[fixtures](/fixtures/)** come straight from Sofascore.
Nothing is retyped here, which means nothing here can be out of date or wrong in a way
Sofascore isn't. Switch divisions, jump between rounds, check the golden boot race.

**[Honours](/honours/)** has the full record. Final tables from every completed season,
cup and Charity Shield winners, and whoever currently holds the title.

**[Teams](/teams/)** lists every studio that has ever entered a side, including the ones
that have since drifted off. If your badge is missing, that's a fixable problem.

**[Media](/media/)** mirrors the Instagram feed, so match photos land here automatically.

## The important page

The one that matters is **[Contact](/contact/)**. The league only works if studios keep
putting sides in, and the single biggest barrier has always been that nobody knew who to ask.
Now you do.

If your studio hasn't got a team: send us a message with a rough headcount and we'll tell you
what the next season looks like. If you're a player without a side, say so, teams are almost
always short, and someone always needs a keeper.
