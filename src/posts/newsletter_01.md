## The Cup

</figure>

Which brings us nicely onto the brand new competition that we have started this season.. The VFL CUP!

If for some reason you do not know what the VFL cup is yet then let me explain it;

This year teams inside and outside of the league will be playing in an 8 aside knockout competition.

Every round of the cup will be drawn randomly. The games will be played the same as league games with the exception that all draws must be decided via a penalty shootout as 1 team has to walk out winners.
