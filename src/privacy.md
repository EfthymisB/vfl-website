## Who we are

The VFX Football League is run by volunteer organisers. We're responsible for the data described
here. For anything about your data, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

The league is for adults: every player must be 18 or over.

## Team registration

Each season, teams register through the [team portal](/portal/).

**Team contacts.** We keep your name and email address so you can sign in and so we can reach
you about the season. If your team marks a free agent as interested, that player sees your team,
your name and your email address, so they can get in touch.

**Players.** The team contact enters each player's full name, date of birth, nationality and
position, and optionally a shirt number, height, preferred foot and a photo. We use these to register the squad and to set
the team up on [Sofascore]({{ site.social.sofascore }}), which runs the league's fixtures, results
and statistics.

Before a squad is submitted, the team contact confirms that every player listed knows about this
and is happy with it. If you're a player and you weren't asked, tell us and we'll take you off.

## Free agents {id=free-agents}

Players looking for a team can join the [free-agent pool](/free-agents/). You sign in with your
email address and give us your name, the positions you play, how often you can play, how
competitive you like it, and optionally a phone number, your current and previous teams, and a
few words about yourself.

**Who sees it.** Your profile is shown to the team contacts of this season's teams, so they can
find players. Your email address and phone number are shown only to the teams that mark you as
interested, and you can see which teams those are on your profile page. If you move to a new team
on loan, your current team's contact can see your name and which team you've joined. The
organisers see everything. We use it only to help you find a team; we rely on your agreement,
which you give when you join and can withdraw by deleting your profile.

When you join a team, that team's contact adds you to their squad, and from then on your details
are handled like any other player's (above).

**Control.** You can edit your profile, take a break from the pool or delete your profile at
any time until you've joined a team.

## Who can see what

- **Squads.** Only the organisers and your own team's contact. Teams can't see each other's
  squads or photos. Photos are kept in private storage: there's no public link to them.
- **Organisers** includes a few people who help run the league and have read-only access to the
  organiser pages. They can see the same details as the organisers, but can't change anything.
- **Free-agent profiles.** As described under [Free agents](#free-agents) above.
- **The public.** What's on Sofascore, this website and our social media (below).

### What ends up public

Player names, photos, dates of birth (or age), nationality, position, shirt number, height and
preferred foot can appear publicly on Sofascore. Once there, they're also covered by Sofascore's own privacy policy. If you
want something changed or removed from Sofascore, ask us and we'll take it up with them.

We also post results, match photos and players' names on this website and on our
[Instagram]({{ site.social.instagram }}) and [LinkedIn]({{ site.social.linkedin }}). If you'd rather not
appear in something we've posted, tell us and we'll take it down.

### Why we're allowed to use it

Running the league, and publishing its results and squads on Sofascore and our own channels, is our
legitimate interest, and it's what you'd expect when you join a league. You can object to any of it:
email us and we'll stop, unless we need something to run the league fairly (for example, a match
result that's already happened). For free agents, we rely on your agreement (see above).

## Services we use

We don't sell your data or use it for marketing. These services handle it for us, and only to
provide their service:

- **Supabase** stores the portal's data, team logos and player photos, in their London data centre.
- **Resend** sends the sign-in emails.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that sign-ins come from a person, not a bot.
- **Web3Forms** passes contact-form messages to our inbox, which is a **Gmail** account.
- **Umami** counts visits to the public pages (see [Cookies and the rest of the site](#cookies)).
- **GitHub Pages** hosts this website and keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses), as
  any web host does. The portal pages also load code from **jsDelivr**.

Some of these are based outside the UK, mainly in the US, so your data may be handled there under
their standard data protection terms.

## How long we keep it

- **Players and free agents.** Player details, photos and free-agent profiles are deleted once the
  season is over and set up on Sofascore. Any exports the organisers made (spreadsheets, photo
  downloads) are deleted at the same time.
- **Team contacts.** Kept while your team plays in the league; ask and we'll delete them sooner.
- **Contact-form messages and emails.** Kept only as long as we need them to deal with what you
  asked.
- What's on Sofascore stays there as the league's record, unless you ask us to have it removed.

## Cookies and the rest of the site {id=cookies}

- **Visitor statistics.** We use [Umami](https://umami.is) to count visits: which pages are read,
  roughly where visitors come from (country, and the site that linked here), the type of device
  and browser, and clicks on a few buttons and links (for example sponsor links, or "Join the
  free-agent pool"). It sets no cookies, doesn't identify you or follow you across other sites,
  and we only see totals. Nothing you type into a form is sent to it. It's switched off on the
  team portal and organiser pages. If your browser blocks it, the site works exactly the same.
- **Cookies.** This site sets no cookies of its own. If you sign in to the team portal,
  your browser stores the sign-in session so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.
- **Tables and fixtures** are live widgets from Sofascore, and the **media page** shows our
  Instagram feed through Behold. These load from those services, which may set their own cookies
  under their own policies.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or object to how
we use it: email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). If you're unhappy with how we've
handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at
[ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).
